Analyze the image quality metrics and initial OCR engine confidence to determine whether the transcription is acceptable, requires pre-processing, or warrants an alternate engine second opinion.

QUALITY SIGNALS EVALUATED:

- estimated_skew_angle: Angle in degrees (|angle| > 2.0 indicates skewed orientation)

ROUTING CRITERIA:

3. Hindi / Devanagari script: Route to EasyOCR with hi+en models.
